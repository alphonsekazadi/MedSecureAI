## Overview

Hypertension is a major cardiovascular risk factor affecting over 1 billion people worldwide. Proper diagnosis and management are crucial for preventing cardiovascular events.

## Classification (2017 ACC/AHA Guidelines)

| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|----------|----------------|---|------------------|
| Normal | <120 | and | <80 |
| Elevated | 120-129 | and | <80 |
| Stage 1 | 130-139 | or | 80-89 |
| Stage 2 | ≥140 | or | ≥90 |
| Hypertensive Crisis | >180 | and/or | >120 |

## Diagnostic Workup

### Initial Assessment
- **Multiple BP measurements** on separate occasions
- **Complete history and physical examination**
- **Home BP monitoring** or 24-hour ambulatory monitoring
- **Assessment of cardiovascular risk factors**

### Laboratory Evaluation
- Complete blood count (CBC)
- Comprehensive metabolic panel
- Lipid profile
- Thyroid function tests
- Urinalysis with microscopy
- ECG

### Additional Testing (if indicated)
- Echocardiogram
- Carotid ultrasound
- Ankle-brachial index
- Renal ultrasound

## Lifestyle Modifications

### DASH Diet Principles
- High in fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- Low in saturated fat, cholesterol, sodium
- Target: <2,300 mg sodium daily (ideal <1,500 mg)

### Physical Activity
- **Aerobic exercise:** 150 minutes moderate intensity weekly
- **Resistance training:** 2-3 sessions per week
- **Weight management:** Target BMI 18.5-24.9 kg/m²

### Other Modifications
- Smoking cessation
- Limited alcohol consumption (≤2 drinks/day men, ≤1 drink/day women)
- Stress management techniques

## Pharmacotherapy

### First-Line Agents
1. **ACE Inhibitors** (e.g., lisinopril, enalapril)
2. **ARBs** (e.g., losartan, valsartan)
3. **Thiazide/Thiazide-like diuretics** (e.g., HCTZ, chlorthalidone)
4. **Calcium channel blockers** (e.g., amlodipine, nifedipine)

### Combination Therapy
- Start combination therapy if BP >20/10 mmHg above goal
- Use fixed-dose combinations to improve adherence
- Avoid ACE inhibitor + ARB combinations

## Target Blood Pressure Goals

### General Population
- **<130/80 mmHg** for most adults
- **<140/90 mmHg** for adults ≥65 years with high burden of comorbidity

### Special Populations
- **Diabetes:** <130/80 mmHg
- **CKD:** <130/80 mmHg
- **CAD:** <130/80 mmHg
- **Stroke/TIA:** <130/80 mmHg

## Monitoring and Follow-up

### Initial Phase
- **Monthly visits** until BP controlled
- **Monitor for side effects** and drug interactions
- **Assess adherence** to medications and lifestyle changes

### Maintenance Phase
- **Every 3-6 months** for stable patients
- **Annual lab monitoring** (electrolytes, creatinine)
- **Home BP monitoring** encouraged

## Hypertensive Emergency Management

### Immediate Assessment
- Neurological examination
- Cardiovascular examination
- Ophthalmoscopic examination
- Assess for end-organ damage

### Treatment Principles
- **Gradual BP reduction:** 10-15% in first hour
- **Avoid sublingual nifedipine**
- **IV agents:** nicardipine, clevidipine, hydralazine
- **Monitor closely** in intensive care setting

---

*Guidelines based on 2017 ACC/AHA High Blood Pressure Clinical Practice Guideline. Always individualize treatment based on patient factors and comorbidities.*
